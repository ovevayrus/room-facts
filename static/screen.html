Room Facts

Standing screens for a 3,000-person developer conference. Each screen shows one big fact about the people nearby. A ticker of smaller facts runs along the bottom and flows from one screen into the next. Screens stand back to back in clusters of 3 or 4, so one faces you from any direction.

    Rules every fact follows

    These keep the screens interesting without pointing at anyone.

    • Only people who opted in are counted. Here that's 1,240 of 3,000 attendees.
    • Never fewer than 3 people. "1 person here is from Pune" points straight at someone, so it never appears.
    • No narrowing combinations. Two details are never combined if together they shrink a group to 1 or 2 people.
    • Names stay hidden. Scanning means "I'm one of them." You only see the others who also scanned and said yes.
    • Rare beats common. A fact about 3 to 40 people gives someone a reason to walk over. "Most people here use Python" goes in the ticker, if anywhere.

    Where facts come from

    • GitHub: public contributions, profile city, account age, live push activity.
    • Two questions at registration: where you grew up, and what you did before tech.
    • Session check-ins: which room you're in, so room screens can talk about the room.
    // Each big fact is one graph query (FalkorDB, Cypher)
MATCH (p:Person {optedIn: true})-[:CONTRIBUTED_TO]->(r:Repo {name: 'fastapi/fastapi'})
WITH count(p) AS n
WHERE n >= 3            // the minimum-group rule
RETURN n
